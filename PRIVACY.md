
# Privacy Policy

## General Information

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. 
This Privacy Policy explains that our software does not collect, store, 
or process any personal data in accordance with the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO).

## Data Collection and Processing

ComfyLoot does not:
- Collect any personal information from users.
- Permanently store any user-related data on servers or local devices.
  - Your collected items will be stored in memory while the software is running, this information will be lost 
- Process or share any personal data with third parties.

In short: No personal data is collected, saved, or analyzed by this software.

## Use of External APIs

ComfyLoot retrieves certain marketboard from the [Universalis](https://universalis.app)-API.
Please note:

The API provides public non-personal data that is used solely for displaying or processing within the application.
We do not transmit any user data to Universalis.
Universalis has not provided a public privacy policy, 
and we therefore cannot guarantee their compliance with GDPR.
If you have concerns about data processing by Universalis,
we recommend refraining from using ComfyLoot until the provider’s data handling practices are clarified.

## Legal Basis for Processing

As no personal data is collected, stored, or processed, the requirements for a legal basis under Article 6 GDPR do not apply.

## Data Storage and Retention

Since no data is collected, no storage or retention of personal information takes place.

## User Rights

Under the GDPR, users have certain rights (e.g., access, rectification, deletion, and objection).
However, as we do not collect or process any personal data, these rights do not apply in this context.


## Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy in case of future changes to our software or applicable data protection laws.
